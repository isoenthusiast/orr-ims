## 1. Use instructions

Use with [Implementation Guide r03](ORR-SOP-IMP-001_IMPLEMENTATION-GUIDE.md).
Copy to an authorized private working location; allocate a unique assessment
record ID and preserve this template ID/revision. This is a blank template,
not evidence of an assessment or a complete list of external requirements.

**M** = mandatory; **C** = conditional when applicable; **O** = optional.
Enter Unknown with owner/next action where unresolved. Not applicable requires
a scope-based reason and reviewer decision. Blank mandatory cells are incomplete.
Repeat rows for every in-scope process and applicable requirement; one example
or sample does not establish complete coverage.

Use states **Not assessed**, **Unknown**, **Gap identified**, **Design present—operation
not verified**, **Sample supports criterion**, or **Not applicable—justified**.
“Sample supports criterion” requires a defined criterion, retrievable evidence,
sample/period and competent review; it is not certification or an unrestricted
effectiveness claim. Use the organization's approved prioritization method; if
absent, record it as a gap rather than inventing numerical risk scores.

Check scope/source coverage, role authority, competence, process handoffs,
control design, tools/access, evidence correction/retention, exception handling,
assurance, management review and improvement. These are implementation prompts,
not a substitute for the applicable authoritative requirement set.

## 5. Filing and correction

Record the private operational index, custodian, access group, retention basis,
hold/disposal authority and storage location before use. Pending arrangements
mean preserve without automatic deletion. Amend records with actor, date, reason
and prior value retained; do not overwrite review decisions. Validate unique IDs,
all mandatory fields, evidence retrieval, justified exclusions and action links.
Do not commit completed organization records into the public repository.

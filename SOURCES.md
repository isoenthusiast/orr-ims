## ORCA

ORCA here means Objective → Risk → Control → Assurance. It is not claimed as an
invention of Edward Wee or this project. Prior use of this meaning is documented
in the 2014 interview with Joop Brakenhoff by Edward L. Baudoin,
[“Heineken Business Framework: een GRC-casus,” Audit Magazine](https://auditmagazine.nl/artikelen/heineken-business-framework-een-grc-casus/).
That is evidence of prior use, not a finding of first authorship, ownership or
permission to copy that article. The article, its diagrams and Heineken's internal
rules are not included. ORCA's ultimate origin and any applicable proprietary
source conditions remain unverified.

# Project governance and release status

Edward Wee is the project maintainer and directs compilation and application
design. On 2026-10-30 he explicitly authorized publication of this separate draft
edition, accepting the recorded unresolved risks. That decision supersedes the
earlier prepublication hold for this selection only. It does not change the
original workspace's private status or authorize release of its source library,
commercial plugins, client information or history.

The publication decision is owner risk acceptance, not an audit pass, a grant of
third-party rights or a legal-compliance certification. The unresolved matters
are the scoped ORR IMS name assessment, independent/competent release review,
and the separate applicability of historical standards-processing terms. No
specific publisher republication-permission requirement was identified in the
reviewed candidate; that is a bounded finding, not comprehensive legal clearance.

This edition contains draft documentation, fictional data and a local brand
preview/builder. It is not an operating IMS, certification service or deployable
application. Controlled document reviewers and organizational approvers remain
unassigned. Codex performed author-led checks; these are not independent audits.
Public availability does not make a draft an effective organizational instruction.

The [distribution inventory](docs/governance/ORR-REG-REL-001_RELEASE-INVENTORY.csv)
and its [guide](docs/governance/ORR-REG-REL-001_INVENTORY-GUIDE.md) identify this
selection. The private preparation index remains the authoring master. Future
changes require source/rights checks, exact version tracking and an attributable
maintainer decision; this authorization is not a blanket release of future work.

Use [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) for proposals and [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)
for reporting. Any future app deployment requires separate security, privacy,
operations, plugin and organization-specific assessments.

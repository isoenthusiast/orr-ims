# Contributing to ORR IMS

This draft project welcomes clear, scoped proposals through GitHub issues and
pull requests. Read [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md) for the draft release decision.

Describe the problem, affected process/documents, proposed change, evidence and
limitations. Use fictional data. Do not submit standards text, publisher figures,
employer/client materials, personal records or credentials without verified
authority. Links and bibliographic references are preferred over copied sources.
Disclose AI assistance and the sources needed to assess a contribution's rights.

For controlled documents follow the
[document-control draft](docs/governance/ORR-STD-DOC-001_DOCUMENT-CONTROL.md):
preserve identity, revision, history, sources, draft status and evidence needs.
For brand changes edit the token master and run the paired builder with `--check`.
Record what was tested; do not equate a local check with independent assurance.

The maintainer reviews content, rights, applicability, compatibility and evidence
before acceptance. No contribution is accepted into a release until its applicable
licence and contributor authority are recorded. Submit original contributions
under the applicable licence in [LICENSING.md](LICENSING.md), identifying exceptions
before review. Do not offer an employer's rights without authority.

Keep discussion respectful and relevant; avoid harassment and disclosure of
private information. A maintainer may restrict or remove content that violates
these expectations. Reporting arrangements are described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
